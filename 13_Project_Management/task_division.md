# Two-person task division

## Person A — sonar, dataset, baseline
- Study SSS imaging geometry, illumination, acoustic-shadow formation, and failure cases.
- Audit MILCO/NOMBO provenance, license, metadata, image context, annotations, and survey grouping.
- Build data manifest and leakage-safe split proposal.
- Implement detector-only baseline after dataset/novelty gates pass.
- Develop conventional highlight/shadow candidate extraction and qualitative examples.

## Person B — literature, annotation, evaluation
- Review critical papers in full text and maintain prior-art matrix.
- Draft annotation definitions and pilot protocol.
- Lead observability and reliability/calibration design.
- Define baselines, metrics, statistics, and experiment log.
- Maintain reproducibility and manuscript references.

## Shared
Both read critical papers, independently annotate a shared pilot subset, measure agreement, challenge the novelty claim, and review results. Do not begin substantial training until full-text novelty checks and dataset audit pass.
